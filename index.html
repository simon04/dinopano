dinopano

360° panorama viewer for Wikimedia Commons

Shows 360° photos from Wikimedia Commons in your browser. Link to a panorama with https://dinopano.toolforge.org/#File_name.jpg, for example:

  • Salzburg Hauptbahnhof
  • Fraueninsel Chiemsee Panorama (aerial)
  • Hiouchi, California (partial panorama)

More panoramas in Category:Spherical panoramics.
